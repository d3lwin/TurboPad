TurboPad controller artwork
===========================

`assets/ds4_front.png` is derived from:

  Dualshock 4 Layout.svg
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dualshock_4_Layout.svg

  Author: Tokyoship
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The original vector artwork was recoloured for TurboPad's dark interface and
rasterised with `tools/build_pad_asset.py`. No other changes were made to the
shape or layout of the controller.

The unmodified source is kept in `assets/ds4_reference.svg` for rebuilds.
